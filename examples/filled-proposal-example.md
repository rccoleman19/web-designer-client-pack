## 2. Understanding

### What you told me

Hearth & Crumb Bakery is a neighborhood bakery in East Austin that sells daily bread, pastries, and weekend catering. Most new customers still find you from a paper menu, Instagram, or walking by the shop. The website is a 2018 WordPress theme that is slow on phones and hard for your staff to update.

The current situation:

- The homepage does not show today’s hours, the allergen note, or that you close when you sell out.
- The menu is a PDF that is often a week behind the case.
- Catering requests come in as unstructured Instagram DMs. You want them in one inbox.
- The photo grid is leftover from a food-blogger visit and does not match the shop as it looks now.

What you want the new site to do:

- Make hours, location, and the current menu obvious on a phone.
- Take catering inquiries with date, headcount, and dietary notes.
- Look current next to nearby bakeries that redesigned in the last two years.
- Let Maya or a manager change hours, menu items, and photos without calling a developer.

### What I am proposing

A redesigned 8-page website on WordPress (block theme) that:

- Works on phones, tablets, and desktops
- Loads at a reasonable speed on a typical broadband connection
- Lets you update hours, menu items, catering lead copy, and photo galleries without writing code
- Uses your existing navy-and-cream palette, the current wordmark, and new photos you will shoot in the shop the week of September 21

This proposal is based on our call on September 4, 2026 and the notes in the shared brief. If I have a fact wrong, say so before we start.

## 3. Scope

### Pages

| # | Page | Purpose | Notes |
|---|------|---------|-------|
| 1 | Home | Hours, today’s highlight, address, Instagram strip | Sell-out banner is a yes/no toggle in the CMS |
| 2 | Menu | Bread, pastry, drinks, weekly specials | Editable items and allergen tags; no online ordering |
| 3 | About | Story, oven, neighborhood | Uses existing copy Maya sent after the call |
| 4 | Catering | Packages + inquiry form | Form emails Maya and catering@hearthandcrumb.example |
| 5 | Order / pickup | How to order in person and by phone | No ecommerce in this quote |
| 6 | Gallery | Shop and product photos | Up to 24 images in the first load |
| 7 | Location | Map, parking, transit note | Embed only; no custom map app |
| 8 | Contact | Form, phone, press email | Same inbox as catering, tagged “general” |

### Features in this quote

- Custom layout on WordPress (block theme on your current host, WP Engine)
- Mobile-first layout for every page in the table
- Contact form and catering form (different subject tags, same inbox)
- Basic on-page setup: title tags, meta descriptions, and heading structure for the eight pages
- Google Analytics 4 install on the existing property
- HTTPS on hearthandcrumb.example (already on; I will confirm after cutover)
- Redirects from the current eight public URLs plus the old `/menu.pdf`
- Handoff: WordPress login plus a short written how-to for hours, menu items, the sell-out banner, and galleries
- Staging URL on a subdomain until launch

### Content and assets you provide

By September 25, 2026 you will send:

- Final written copy for About, Catering, and Order / pickup (Home and Menu can start from what is live now)
- Wordmark (SVG) and the navy / cream / warm-white values you already use
- At least 16 photos you have rights to use, shot after the new pastry case is in. If that shoot slips, we launch with a smaller gallery and swap images later at no extra page fee.
- Access to WP Engine, the domain registrar (Hover), and the current WordPress admin
- Maya as primary contact, with a reply target of 2 business days

Late content pauses the calendar. The end date moves by the same number of business days the files are late.
