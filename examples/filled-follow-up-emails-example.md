# Follow-up emails (filled example)

> **Example only.** Hearth & Crumb Bakery, Hale Studio, and the dates below are fictional. They match `filled-proposal-example.md`. Do not send these as-is. Swap in your lead’s name, dates, and numbers.

Suggested gaps for this file: A on inquiry (September 3), B when the proposal goes out (September 8), C on September 11, D on September 15.

---

## A. Lead reply after inquiry

**When:** September 3, 2026 (same day as the form note)

**Subject:** Re: Website project for Hearth & Crumb Bakery

Hi Maya,

Thanks for writing. I design and build websites for small food businesses, and I have time to look at a redesign for Hearth & Crumb.

To send a useful proposal I need a few details:

1. What should the new site do that the current one does not?
2. Rough page list if you have one — or “not sure yet” is fine.
3. Do you have a target launch month?
4. Do you already have copy and photos, or will those need to be created?
5. Is there a budget range you are working within? A range helps me recommend the right package instead of guessing.

I have a 20-minute call open on Thursday, September 4 at 10:00 a.m. or 2:30 p.m. Central. If neither works, send two times that do.

I will follow up with a written proposal after we talk. No charge for the call.
