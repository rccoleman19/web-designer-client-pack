## 5. Revisions

- Design phase includes **1 round** of revisions to the approved direction.
- Pre-launch build includes **2 rounds** of revisions to the staging site.
- A “round” is one grouped list of written comments, not a running chat of one-off tweaks.
- Comments are due within 3 business days of Designer sending a review link.
- Changes that add pages, features, or integrations, or that reverse an approved direction, are out of scope and billed as a change order.
- Extra revision rounds: $275 each.

Silence after the feedback window may be treated as approval so the calendar can move.

This section is a starting point. It is not a substitute for a lawyer-drafted IP clause.

## 7. Cancellation

- Either party may cancel in writing.
- If Client cancels after signing: Client pays for work completed through the cancel date, against the deposit and any progress invoices, at $125/hour or a reasonable share of the remaining milestones, whichever Designer states in the cancel note. Completed work already invoiced stays due.
- The deposit is earned once kickoff has occurred (access requested and sitemap or kickoff notes sent). Unused deposit after completed-work charges is refunded within 10 business days. If completed work exceeds the deposit, Designer will invoice the difference.
- If Designer cancels (illness, over-booking error, or inability to deliver): Designer refunds fees for undelivered milestones and hands over work product paid for to date. Designer is not liable for Client’s lost sales, ads, or other indirect costs.
- Neither party is responsible for delay caused by outages, illness, or events outside reasonable control. Dates extend by the length of the delay.

Cap on Designer’s liability under this SOW: fees paid by Client under this SOW in the 3 months before the claim. This is a template limit, not a guarantee it will hold in every state.

## 8. Other terms

- **Independent contractor.** Designer is not an employee of Client.
- **Confidentiality.** Both sides will keep non-public business information private, except as needed to do the work or as required by law.
- **Warranty.** Designer will fix defects that are clearly caused by Designer’s work and reported in writing within 14 days after launch. This is a repair promise, not a promise about traffic, sales, search rank, or uptime of third-party hosts.
- **Hosting and security.** After handoff, Client (or Client’s host) is responsible for updates, backups, and security unless a separate care plan is signed.
- **Governing law.** Texas, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Venue: Travis County, Texas.
- **Entire agreement.** This SOW and the approved proposal are the full agreement for this project. If they conflict, this SOW controls. Changes must be in writing (email is enough).
