# Follow-up emails

Four short emails for a typical website lead. Copy, replace the brackets, and send from your own address.

Keep one thread per lead when you can. If you use a form-to-inbox tool, reply to that thread for Email A.

Do not stack these on the same day. Suggested gaps: A on inquiry, B when the proposal goes out, C about 3 days after B, D about 7 days after B (or 4 days after C).

---

## A. Lead reply after inquiry

**When:** Same business day if you can, next business morning if not.

**Subject:** Re: [THEIR SUBJECT or “Website project for [CLIENT COMPANY]”]

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

Thanks for writing. I design and build websites for [YOUR NICHE OR “small businesses”], and I have time to look at [PROJECT TYPE — new site / redesign] for [CLIENT COMPANY].

To send a useful proposal I need a few details:

1. What should the new site do that the current one does not? (Or, if this is a first site: what should a visitor be able to do in the first visit?)
2. Rough page list if you have one — or “not sure yet” is fine.
3. Do you have a target launch month?
4. Do you already have copy and photos, or will those need to be created?
5. Is there a budget range you are working within? A range helps me recommend the right package instead of guessing.

I have a [CALL LENGTH, e.g. 20]-minute call open on [DATE/TIME OPTION 1] and [DATE/TIME OPTION 2] [TIME ZONE]. If neither works, send two times that do.

I will follow up with a written proposal after we talk. No charge for the call.

[YOUR NAME]  
[YOUR STUDIO NAME]  
[YOUR PHONE] · [YOUR SITE URL]

## B. Proposal sent

**When:** Same day you send the proposal PDF or link.

**Subject:** Proposal for [CLIENT COMPANY] website — valid through [VALID THROUGH DATE]

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

The proposal for [CLIENT COMPANY] is attached / here: [PROPOSAL LINK].

In short:

- Scope: [ONE LINE — e.g. 8-page redesign on WordPress, contact + catering forms]
- Timeline: [TIMELINE RANGE] after kickoff and content
- Investment: $[PROJECT TOTAL] (deposit $[DEPOSIT AMOUNT] to start)

The number is good through [VALID THROUGH DATE]. After that I will re-quote if we have not signed.

If you want a smaller first version or a different page list, reply with what to change and I will send a revised total.

Next step if it looks right: I send a statement of work, you sign, and we book kickoff after the deposit.

Questions are welcome. I am around [YOUR TYPICAL HOURS] [TIME ZONE].

[YOUR NAME]  
[YOUR STUDIO NAME]  
[YOUR PHONE]

## C. Soft nudge (~3 days later)

**When:** About 3 days after Email B if you have had no reply. Do not add new discounts here.

**Subject:** Checking in on the [CLIENT COMPANY] proposal

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

Quick check-in on the [PROJECT TYPE] proposal I sent on [PROPOSAL DATE].

I am still able to start the week of [HOLD START WEEK] if we sign by [HOLD UNTIL DATE]. After that I will put the next lead on that slot.

If the scope is close but not quite right, tell me what to cut or add. A shorter page list is a common fix.

If the timing is wrong, a one-line “not now” is enough. I will close the file and you can write again later.

[YOUR NAME]  
[YOUR STUDIO NAME]  
[YOUR PHONE]

---

## D. Last call (~7 days)

**When:** About 7 days after Email B, or when the proposal expires — whichever comes first. Then stop unless they reply.

**Subject:** Last note on the [CLIENT COMPANY] website proposal

Hi [CLIENT FIRST NAME],

This is my last note on the proposal dated [PROPOSAL DATE] (valid through [VALID THROUGH DATE]).

I will close this file on [CLOSE DATE] so I can plan the rest of the month. If you still want to move, reply this week and I will send the statement of work.

If you hired someone else or paused the project, no need to explain. I will assume it is closed after [CLOSE DATE].

Thanks for the time on our call.

[YOUR NAME]  
[YOUR STUDIO NAME]  
[YOUR PHONE]

## Optional one-liners

Use these inside a reply, not as extra chase emails.

- **They asked for a lower price:** “I can lower the total by cutting [PAGE OR FEATURE]. I do not discount the same scope.”
- **They went quiet after “looks good”:** “Ready to send the SOW when you are. Deposit is $[DEPOSIT AMOUNT].”
- **They want to start later:** “I can hold a kickoff the week of [LATER WEEK] if we sign now and put the deposit on [HOLD TERMS]. Or we can re-quote closer to that month.”

Do not invent urgency you cannot keep. If the calendar is open, say so.
