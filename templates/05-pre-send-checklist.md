# Pre-send checklist

Run this before you send a proposal or pricing sheet. Ten minutes now avoids a messy revision later.

Check a box only when it is true for *this* lead.

## Fit

- [ ] I had a call or a written brief. I am not quoting from a one-line form only.
- [ ] I know the project type: landing page, multi-page new site, or redesign.
- [ ] I wrote down what “done” means in one sentence: [DONE SENTENCE].
- [ ] The page count in the proposal matches the page table, the pricing sheet, and my notes.

## Numbers and dates

- [ ] Every `[BRACKET]` is replaced or deleted. None left for the client to see.
- [ ] Package price, add-ons, and total add up. Deposit percent matches the dollar amount.
- [ ] Timeline range matches my real calendar for the next [LOOKAHEAD WEEKS] weeks.
- [ ] “Valid through” and any hold date are dates I can keep.
- [ ] Payment methods listed are ones I actually accept.

## Scope

- [ ] Features in the quote are ones I will build. No “maybe” items left in.
- [ ] “What’s not included” lists the usual extras this client might assume (copy, photos, SEO retainer, ecommerce, extra revision rounds).
- [ ] I named the platform and who owns hosting after launch.
- [ ] I named what the client must send and by when.
- [ ] Revision rounds are a number, not “until you’re happy.”

## Files and names

- [ ] Client name, company, and email are spelled the way they write them.
- [ ] Proposal ID / date in the filename matches the cover: `[PROPOSAL ID] [CLIENT COMPANY] proposal`.
- [ ] If I attach a pricing sheet, the selected package and total match the proposal.
- [ ] Links (proposal, portfolio piece, calendar) open in a logged-out browser.

## Tone

- [ ] I did not promise traffic, rank, leads, or revenue.
- [ ] I did not invent a legal guarantee.
- [ ] I did not imply I have a team if it is only me.
- [ ] Next step is one action: reply, book a call, or sign.

## Send

- [ ] PDF or share link is the final version, not a draft with comments.
- [ ] Email B subject line uses their company name and the valid-through date.
- [ ] I set a reminder for the 3-day nudge and the 7-day last call.
- [ ] I logged the lead on my list: name, date sent, total, hold date.

If any item is unchecked, fix it before you send. A short delay is better than a proposal you cannot deliver.
