## 6. Intellectual property

- **Before final payment:** Designer owns the work product. Client has a limited right to review it on staging.
- **After final payment in full:** Designer assigns to Client the custom design and custom code produced under this SOW, excluding third-party tools, fonts, stock, plugins, frameworks, and Designer’s pre-existing libraries and starter kits.
- Client must keep valid licenses for those third-party items.
- Designer may show the finished site, screenshots, and a short project summary in a portfolio and on social profiles unless Client objects in writing before launch.
- Client warrants that materials it provides (copy, photos, logos, fonts) are owned by Client or licensed for this use, and that Client will handle any claim that arises from those materials.

This section is a starting point. It is not a substitute for a lawyer-drafted IP clause.

## 7. Cancellation

- Either party may cancel in writing.
- If Client cancels after signing: Client pays for work completed through the cancel date, against the deposit and any progress invoices, at [HOURLY RATE] or a reasonable share of the remaining milestones, whichever Designer states in the cancel note. Completed work already invoiced stays due.
- The deposit is earned once kickoff has occurred (access requested and sitemap or kickoff notes sent). Unused deposit after completed-work charges is refunded within [REFUND DAYS] business days. If completed work exceeds the deposit, Designer will invoice the difference.
- If Designer cancels (illness, over-booking error, or inability to deliver): Designer refunds fees for undelivered milestones and hands over work product paid for to date. Designer is not liable for Client’s lost sales, ads, or other indirect costs.
- Neither party is responsible for delay caused by outages, illness, or events outside reasonable control. Dates extend by the length of the delay.

Cap on Designer’s liability under this SOW: fees paid by Client under this SOW in the [LIABILITY LOOKBACK, e.g. 3 months] before the claim. This is a template limit, not a guarantee it will hold in every state.
