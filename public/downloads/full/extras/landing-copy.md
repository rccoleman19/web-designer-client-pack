## FAQ

### Do you refund?

These are digital goods. No refunds after download. If a file is missing or will not open, email [SUPPORT EMAIL] within 7 days of purchase and I will replace the file.

### Will this get me more clients or more revenue?

No claim is made about clients, close rate, or income. This is a set of documents. Results depend on your work, your prices, and the leads you already have.

### Is the statement of work legal advice?

No. It is a working draft. Have a lawyer in your state review payment, revisions, intellectual property, and cancellation before you use it with paying clients.

### Can I put my studio name on the templates?

Yes. That is the point. Replace the brackets and send them as yours.

### Are the bakery files a real project?

No. Hearth & Crumb Bakery and the dollar amounts are labeled examples so you can see a completed proposal. Do not show them as your past work.

### What format are the files?

Markdown. Open them in any text editor, Notion, Google Docs, or a Markdown-to-PDF tool you already use.

### Do I need a special platform?

No. The templates are platform-agnostic. You fill in WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, or static HTML where the field asks for `[PLATFORM]`.

### Can I use this if I am not in the United States?

The language is US English and the SOW assumes a US-style independent-contractor setup. If you work under another country’s rules, have local counsel adapt the SOW. The proposal, pricing sheet, emails, and checklist still work as drafts.
